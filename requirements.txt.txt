A **CRM System (Customer Relationship Management System)** should help a business manage leads, customers, communication, sales opportunities, follow-ups, deals, tasks, and customer history in one organized platform.

## 1. Lead Management

Capture and manage potential customers from different sources such as website forms, calls, WhatsApp, social media, referrals, campaigns, and walk-ins.

## 2. Customer Management

Store full customer profiles including name, company, phone number, email, address, industry, notes, history, and relationship status.

## 3. Contact Management

Manage multiple contacts under one company or account, including decision-makers, finance contacts, managers, and technical contacts.

## 4. Company / Account Management

Create company accounts with business details, tax information, billing details, contacts, deals, projects, invoices, and communication history.

## 5. Sales Pipeline

Track deals through stages such as new lead, contacted, qualified, proposal sent, negotiation, won, lost, or follow-up later.

## 6. Deal / Opportunity Management

Create sales opportunities with deal value, expected closing date, probability, source, assigned salesperson, status, and related documents.

## 7. Lead Qualification

Score or categorize leads based on interest level, budget, urgency, decision authority, company size, source, and likelihood to buy.

## 8. Follow-up Management

Schedule follow-ups, calls, meetings, reminders, and next actions for each lead or customer.

## 9. Task Management

Assign tasks to sales, support, marketing, or management teams with deadlines, priorities, status, and notes.

## 10. Activity Timeline

Show a complete timeline of calls, messages, emails, meetings, notes, proposals, invoices, tasks, and status changes for each customer.

## 11. Communication History

Record all communication with leads and customers, including phone calls, emails, WhatsApp messages, SMS, meetings, and internal notes.

## 12. Email Integration

Connect email accounts to send, receive, and track customer emails directly from the CRM.

## 13. WhatsApp / SMS Integration

Send messages, reminders, quotations, offers, and follow-ups through WhatsApp or SMS with saved templates.

## 14. Call Logging

Log phone calls manually or through phone integration, including call result, duration, notes, and next action.

## 15. Meeting Management

Schedule customer meetings, link them to leads or deals, add notes, and track meeting outcomes.

## 16. Calendar Integration

Sync tasks, meetings, follow-ups, reminders, and appointments with a calendar.

## 17. Quotation and Proposal Management

Create or attach quotations, proposals, pricing documents, contracts, and commercial offers related to a deal.

## 18. Quotation-to-Deal Linking

Link each quotation or proposal to a specific lead, customer, opportunity, or sales stage.

## 19. Customer Segmentation

Group customers by industry, location, source, value, status, interest, purchase history, company size, or engagement level.

## 20. Lead Source Tracking

Track where each lead came from, such as ads, organic search, referral, event, website, social media, direct call, or salesperson.

## 21. Campaign Tracking

Track marketing campaigns and measure how many leads, deals, and customers each campaign generated.

## 22. Customer Status Tracking

Use statuses such as new lead, contacted, qualified, active customer, inactive customer, lost customer, VIP, blacklisted, or archived.

## 23. Customer Notes

Allow staff to add internal notes, important details, preferences, objections, requirements, and relationship history.

## 24. Sales Forecasting

Estimate expected revenue based on open deals, deal probability, expected closing dates, and sales pipeline value.

## 25. Revenue Tracking

Track revenue by customer, deal, salesperson, product, service, campaign, branch, or time period.

## 26. Customer Support Tickets

Create and manage customer support requests, complaints, issues, service tickets, and resolution history.

## 27. Ticket Status Management

Track support statuses such as open, pending, in progress, resolved, closed, escalated, or waiting for customer.

## 28. Customer Feedback

Collect customer ratings, comments, satisfaction scores, complaints, and suggestions.

## 29. Document Management

Upload and store customer-related documents such as contracts, proposals, invoices, receipts, agreements, project files, and ID documents.

## 30. Automation Rules

Automate repetitive work such as assigning leads, sending welcome messages, creating follow-up tasks, updating statuses, and sending reminders.

## 31. Lead Assignment

Automatically or manually assign leads to salespeople based on branch, source, industry, region, workload, or rotation.

## 32. Notifications and Reminders

Notify users about new leads, overdue follow-ups, upcoming meetings, pending tasks, deal changes, customer replies, and unpaid invoices.

## 33. Sales Team Management

Manage sales representatives, targets, assigned leads, activities, performance, commissions, and pipeline progress.

## 34. Role-Based Permissions

Control access for admin, manager, salesperson, support agent, accountant, marketing team, and viewer roles.

## 35. Dashboard

Show key information such as new leads, open deals, won deals, lost deals, revenue, overdue follow-ups, sales performance, and conversion rates.

## 36. Reports and Analytics

Generate reports for leads, deals, sales, revenue, conversions, sources, campaigns, customer activity, support tickets, and team performance.

## 37. Conversion Rate Reports

Measure how many leads become qualified leads, opportunities, customers, and successful sales.

## 38. Lost Deal Analysis

Record why deals are lost, such as price, timing, competitor, no response, budget issue, or wrong fit.

## 39. Customer Retention Tracking

Track repeat customers, inactive customers, renewal opportunities, upsell opportunities, and customer lifetime value.

## 40. Upsell and Cross-sell Management

Identify opportunities to sell additional products, services, upgrades, renewals, or packages to existing customers.

## 41. Contract and Renewal Tracking

Track contract start dates, expiry dates, renewal dates, service periods, and renewal reminders.

## 42. Customer Portal

Allow customers to view their requests, quotations, invoices, tickets, documents, and communication history if needed.

## 43. Product and Service Catalog

Store products, services, packages, prices, descriptions, and categories for use in deals, quotations, and proposals.

## 44. Invoice and Payment Linking

Link invoices, receipts, payments, outstanding balances, and financial records to the related customer or deal.

## 45. Integration with Accounting

Sync customer data, invoices, payments, balances, taxes, and revenue with the accounting system.

## 46. Integration with Website Forms

Capture website inquiries automatically and create leads inside the CRM.

## 47. Integration with Social Media

Capture or track inquiries from platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, or other channels.

## 48. Integration with Marketing Tools

Connect CRM data with email marketing, SMS marketing, ad campaigns, landing pages, and automation tools.

## 49. Import and Export

Import leads and customers from Excel or CSV and export reports, contact lists, pipeline data, and customer records.

## 50. Search and Filtering

Search by customer name, phone number, company, email, lead source, salesperson, status, deal value, date, or location.

## 51. Duplicate Detection

Warn users when a customer, company, phone number, or email already exists to avoid duplicate records.

## 52. Custom Fields

Allow businesses to add fields specific to their industry, such as project type, customer category, contract number, preferred service, or company size.

## 53. Multi-Branch Support

Support different branches, teams, territories, sales regions, or business units with centralized reporting.

## 54. Multi-Language Support

Allow users to work in multiple languages and generate customer-facing documents in different languages if needed.

## 55. Mobile Access

Allow sales and support teams to access customer data, update tasks, log calls, and manage deals from mobile or tablet.

## 56. Audit Log

Track who created, edited, deleted, assigned, converted, contacted, or changed customer and deal records.

## 57. Security and Backup

Protect customer data with secure login, role permissions, encrypted storage, backups, and recovery options.

## 58. Data Privacy and Consent

Store customer consent for communication, marketing permissions, unsubscribe requests, and privacy-related records.

## 59. Blacklist / Do Not Contact List

Mark customers or contacts who should not be contacted because of complaints, legal reasons, spam requests, or management decision.

## 60. Archive and Data Retention

Archive old leads, lost deals, inactive customers, and historical records while keeping them searchable.

The most important CRM requirements are **lead management, customer profiles, sales pipeline tracking, follow-up reminders, communication history, deal management, reporting, automation, permissions, and integrations with communication channels**.
